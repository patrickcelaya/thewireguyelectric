How it works

Four steps. Zero surprises.

  1. 1

    Call Emma or book a free estimate

    Emma is ready for your call Monday through Friday, 9 am to 5 pm. Prefer to type? Request a free estimate online.

    Get one flat-rate quote

    We'll come out, look at the job, and give you a straight answer on what it'll cost. That's what you pay, whether the job takes one hour or three.

Good questions

Questions neighbors ask us

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes we do! We'll come out, look at the job, and give you a straight answer on what it'll cost. No pressure and no obligation. Contact us today to get a free quote.

What cities do you service?

We travel all over the Phoenix Valley to over 11 different cities and areas including Mesa, Gilbert, Chandler, Scottsdale, Phoenix, Tempe, Queen Creek, San Tan Valley, Apache Junction, Fountain Hills, and Ahwatukee. If you don't see your city listed, give us a call and we'll let you know if you're in our service radius.

Do you have any hidden fees?

Nope! Our technicians understand the importance of upfront pricing. What you see on the invoice is what you get. If any changes are made to your project, we will update it promptly and send it over for you to review.

Do you warranty your work?

Yes, we do. We offer a 2-year warranty on services we provide. Contact us for full warranty details.

Are you licensed, bonded, and insured?

Yes. The Wire Guy Electric is fully licensed, bonded, and insured in the state of Arizona. Our ROC license number is 365306, which you can verify at azroc.gov. This protects you as the homeowner because our work is backed by the state's contractor recovery fund, and our insurance covers any accidents or damage on the job.
