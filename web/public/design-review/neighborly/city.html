Do you offer free estimates?

Do I need a permit for electrical work in Arizona?

For most electrical work beyond basic repairs, yes. Panel upgrades, new circuit installations, rewiring, and EV charger installations all require a permit and inspection in Maricopa County. At The Wire Guy Electric, we handle the entire permit process for you. Any electrician who suggests skipping the permit is someone you should avoid.

Can I install an EV charger myself or do I need an electrician?

You need a licensed electrician. Installing a Level 2 EV charger requires running a dedicated 240-volt circuit from your panel, which involves working inside your breaker panel. It requires a permit and inspection in Arizona, and doing it incorrectly can damage your vehicle's charging system or create a fire hazard. We install chargers for every major EV brand and handle the permit process.

Do you work on weekends or offer emergency service?

Our regular office hours are Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM. If you have an urgent situation, give us a call at 602-769-7892 and we'll do our best to accommodate you. For true emergencies like sparking outlets, burning smells, or a panel that's hot to the touch, call us right away or call 911 if there's an immediate safety threat.

Are you licensed, bonded, and insured?

Yes. The Wire Guy Electric is fully licensed, bonded, and insured in the state of Arizona. Our ROC license number is 365306, which you can verify at azroc.gov. This protects you as the homeowner because our work is backed by the state's contractor recovery fund, and our insurance covers any accidents or damage on the job.
