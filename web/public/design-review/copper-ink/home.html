04 How it works

Four steps. No surprises.

The same clean run on every job, whether it’s one outlet or a whole service upgrade.

  1. 1

    Free estimate

    We’ll come out, look at the job, and give you a straight answer on what it’ll cost. No pressure and no obligation.

  2. 2

    Flat-rate quote

    We quote you a price before we start and that’s what you pay. Whether the job takes us one hour or three, your price stays the same.

  3. 3

    Permit + SRP/APS coordination

    We pull the permit, schedule the inspection, and make sure everything passes. On panel work, we coordinate the meter with APS or SRP.

    Done right

    Labeled breakers, straight wire runs, proper torquing, and no loose neutrals. Backed by our 2-year warranty.

09 Questions

Good questions. Straight answers.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes we do! Contact us today to get a free quote. We'll come out, look at the job, and give you a straight answer on what it'll cost. No pressure and no obligation.

Do you have any hidden fees?

Nope! Our technicians understand the importance of upfront pricing. What you see on the invoice is what you get. If any changes are made to your project, we will update it promptly and send it over for you to review.

Are you licensed, bonded, and insured?

Yes. The Wire Guy Electric is fully licensed, bonded, and insured in the state of Arizona. Our ROC (Registrar of Contractors) license number is 365306, which you can verify at azroc.gov. This protects you as the homeowner because it means our work is backed by the state's contractor recovery fund, and our insurance covers any accidents or damage on the job. Never hire an electrician who can't provide proof of licensing and insurance. Learn more about us.

Do you work on weekends or offer emergency service?

Our regular office hours are Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM. However, we understand that electrical issues don't always happen during business hours. If you have an urgent situation, give us a call at 602-769-7892 and we'll do our best to accommodate you. For true emergencies like sparking outlets, burning smells, or a panel that's hot to the touch, don't wait. Call us right away or call 911 if there's an immediate safety threat. You can also reach us through our contact page.

Do you warranty your work?

Yes, we do. We offer a 2-year warranty on services we provide. Contact us for full warranty details.
