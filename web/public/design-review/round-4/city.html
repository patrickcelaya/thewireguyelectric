04 Questions

Gilbert electrical questions

Utility, permits, estimates and pricing: straight answers before you call.

All questions on our FAQ

Who provides electric service in Gilbert?

Most of Gilbert is in SRP’s electric service area. APS serves a pocket between Guadalupe and Warner roads, from Cooper Road to Lindsay Road.

Who issues electrical permits in Gilbert?

Residential electrical permits in Gilbert are issued by the Town of Gilbert’s Development Services department (Plan Review & Inspection). Applications, fees and inspection requests go through the Town’s OneStopShop portal.

Do I need a permit for electrical work in Arizona?

Permits are required for all major electrical work. At The Wire Guy Electric, we can handle the entire permitting process for you. We pull the permit, schedule the inspection, and make sure everything passes.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes we do! Contact us today to get a free quote. We'll come out, look at the job, and give you a straight answer on what it'll cost. No pressure and no obligation.

Do you have any hidden fees?

Nope! Our technicians understand the importance of upfront pricing. What you see on the invoice is what you get. If any changes are made to your project, we will update it promptly and send it over for you to review.
