Do you offer free estimates?

Yes we do! Contact us today to get a free quote. We'll come out, look at the job, and give you a straight answer on what it'll cost. No pressure and no obligation.

How much does it cost to install an EV charger at home?

Installing a Level 2 EV charger (240-volt) at a home in the Phoenix area typically costs between $750 and $3,500 depending on the distance from your panel to the charging location and whether your panel has room for a new circuit. If your panel needs an upgrade to support the charger, that adds to the cost. We install chargers for all makes and models. Learn more about our EV charger installation service or give us a call for a free estimate.

Are you licensed, bonded, and insured?

Yes. The Wire Guy Electric is fully licensed, bonded, and insured in the state of Arizona. Our ROC (Registrar of Contractors) license number is 365306, which you can verify on the AZ ROC site.

Do you work on weekends or offer emergency service?

Our regular office hours are Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM. If you have an urgent situation, give us a call at 602-769-7892 and we'll do our best to accommodate you. For true emergencies like sparking outlets, burning smells, or a panel that's hot to the touch, call us right away or call 911 if there's an immediate safety threat.

Do I need a permit for electrical work in Arizona?

Permits are required for all major electrical work. At The Wire Guy Electric, we can handle the entire permitting process for you. We pull the permit, schedule the inspection, and make sure everything passes.

What cities do you service?

We travel all over the Phoenix Valley to over 11 different cities and areas including Mesa, Gilbert, Chandler, Scottsdale, Phoenix, Tempe, Queen Creek, San Tan Valley, Apache Junction, Fountain Hills, and Ahwatukee. If you don't see your city listed, don't worry! Just give us a call and we'll let you know if you're in our service radius.
