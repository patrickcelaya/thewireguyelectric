How a Wire Guy job runs

Four steps, one crew, one price. You'll know who's coming, what it costs and when it's done.

  1. 01 Free estimate

    Call Emma or request online

    We'll come out, look at the job, and give you a straight answer on what it'll cost. No pressure and no obligation.

  2. 02 Flat-rate quote

    One price, before we start

    Whether the job takes us one hour or three, your price stays the same. If anything changes, we update it promptly and send it over for you to review.

    Paperwork and utility, handled

    We pull the permit, schedule the inspection, and make sure everything passes. On service upgrades we coordinate the meter work with APS or SRP.

    Done right the first time

    We don't go home until your problem is fixed, we clean up after ourselves, and every job is backed by our 2-year warranty.

Quick answers

Before you call

Are you licensed, bonded, and insured?

Yes. The Wire Guy Electric is fully licensed, bonded, and insured in the state of Arizona. Our ROC (Registrar of Contractors) license number is 365306, which you can verify on the AZ ROC site. This protects you as the homeowner because it means our work is backed by the state's contractor recovery fund, and our insurance covers any accidents or damage on the job.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes we do! Contact us today to get a free quote. We'll come out, look at the job, and give you a straight answer on what it'll cost. No pressure and no obligation.

What cities do you service?

We travel all over the Phoenix Valley to over 11 different cities and areas including Mesa, Gilbert, Chandler, Scottsdale, Phoenix, Tempe, Queen Creek, San Tan Valley, Apache Junction, Fountain Hills, and Ahwatukee. If you don't see your city listed, don't worry! Just give us a call and we'll let you know if you're in our service radius.

Do you warranty your work?

Yes, we do. We offer a 2-year warranty on services we provide. Contact us for full warranty details.

Do you have any hidden fees?

Nope! Our technicians understand the importance of upfront pricing. What you see on the invoice is what you get. If any changes are made to your project, we will update it promptly and send it over for you to review.
