Customers frequently ask

I want to upgrade my lighting, but I don’t know what to do. Can you help me with the design?


Absolutely. We can help plan fixture types, placement, color temperature, dimming, and layered lighting around how you use the room. Contact us today to schedule a free estimate and turn the plan into a code-compliant installation.

Do you have any hidden fees?


Nope! Our technicians understand the importance of upfront pricing. What you see on the invoice is what you get. If any changes are made to your project, we will update it promptly and send it over for you to review.

Are there any services you don’t offer?


We do most electrical work, but not all. Here are some services we don't currently offer: generator installs, solar installs, hanging Christmas/bistro lights, standing lamp repairs, ethernet/data wiring, fiber internet wiring, speaker installs, camera installs, camera doorbell installs, and thermostats. Check out our full services page to see everything we do offer.

Can you tell me more about your business?


We have been in business for over 9 years and take pride in being a local, family-run business. Our customers' happiness and our quality of work are our #1 priorities. We are licensed, bonded, and insured (ROC #365306). While we have slowly been growing our company, we are still a small business. We believe this allows us to offer a more personalized experience for our customers. Learn more on our About Us page or meet our team.

Do you warranty your work?


Yes, we do. We offer a 2-year warranty on services we provide. Contact us for full warranty details.

I have another non-electrical project but don’t know where to start. Can you refer me to someone?


Yes! We are happy to provide referrals for other services to our customers. Over the years we've built relationships with great contractors across the valley in plumbing, HVAC, roofing, and more. Just reach out and we'll point you in the right direction.
